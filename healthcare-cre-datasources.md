## UNFAIR ADVANTAGE SOURCES (Most Analysts Ignore)

**NIH RePORTER API for Life Sciences Leading Indicator:** Most CRE analysts track lab vacancy reactively from broker reports. The NIH RePORTER API, queried weekly with `org_city` filters, gives a real-time 18-month forward signal: when NIH grant flow to a cluster accelerates, lab demand follows within 1–2 leasing cycles. Run rolling 4-quarter grant totals by MSA; a >15% YoY increase has preceded lab absorption spikes in Boston (2017–2019) and San Diego (2020–2022) by 12–18 months. Free, weekly data, no broker intermediary.

**NIC MAP Quarterly Press Releases for Senior Housing:** The full NIC MAP Vision platform costs $15,000–$25,000/year. However, NIC publishes its top-line findings — national occupancy, Top 31 MSA absorption, construction starts — in free quarterly press releases 2 weeks after quarter close. These are indexed by Google and typically sufficient for macro portfolio-level underwriting of Welltower/Ventas assets. Combined with WELL/VTR 10-Q same-store SHOP data (free, EDGAR), an analyst can reconstruct 80% of the senior housing market picture for $0.

**State Cannabis Licensing Databases:** Cannabis CRE analysts universally cite MJBizDaily and IIPR filings, but the direct state licensing portals (CA DCC Socrata API, MA CCC Socrata API, WA WSLCB Socrata API) give daily geocoded license data enabling proprietary analysis: license density by ZIP code, license attrition rates (surrenders/revocations as financial distress signals), and pipeline approval velocity. These are the actual ground-truth datasets that IIPR's tenant revenue depends on — yet almost no CRE researchers query them directly.

## GAP ANALYSIS: GATED DATA AND CHEAPEST LEGITIMATE PATHS

The most critical gated data in healthcare/specialty CRE falls into three tiers. **NIC MAP Vision full** (property-level senior housing data, actual rates, operator-specific metrics) is the most significant gap — at $15,000–$25,000/year it's beyond individual investor budgets, but a consortium approach (Israeli family office LP club pooling access) or subscribing via a US operating partner brings the per-investor cost to $1,500–$3,000. The cheapest legitimate path is pairing NIC's free quarterly press releases with WELL/VTR/BKD 10-Q same-store SHOP disclosures, which reconstruct 70–80% of the market picture. **Definitive Healthcare full platform** (provider intelligence, physician affiliations, claims data) runs $25,000–$75,000/year; the free path is combining the CMS Physician Downloadable File (2.4M providers, free), CMS Open Payments (NPI-level financial relationships, free), and CMS Cost Reports (facility financials, free) — three datasets that together replicate roughly 60% of Definitive's core use cases for MOB underwriting. **STR senior housing benchmarking** and full **CoStar/Real Capital Analytics** transaction data remain genuinely irreplaceable for deal-level comp analysis; the cheapest path is using EDGAR XBRL acquisition disclosures (all public REIT transactions are disclosed with cap rates and price/unit) as a proxy transaction database, supplemented by CBRE/JLL/Newmark free market reports for asking-rate context. For cannabis CRE, the only true gap is consolidated multi-state operator (MSO) financial data — private MSOs (Trulieve, Curaleaf, Green Thumb) file with CSE (Canadian Securities Exchange), and their MD&As are free to download, providing revenue-per-state data that validates IIPR tenant lease coverage at zero cost. A determined analyst team willing to spend 40 hours/quarter building these pipelines can access roughly 75% of what a $100,000/year institutional data budget would buy.
